Q1(a)3 MARKSDefine centroid, centre of gravity and axis of symmetry.Centroid, centre of gravity અને axis of symmetry વ્યાખ્યાયિત કરો.

Centroid: The geometrical centre of an area, line or volume; the first moments of area about centroidal axes are zero for a plane area.

Centre of gravity: The point through which the resultant gravitational force (weight) of a body acts for the given gravitational field.

Axis of symmetry: A line about which a plane figure is divided into two mirror-image halves. The centroid lies on every axis of symmetry. A homogeneous body in a uniform gravitational field has its centre of gravity at its centroid.

Centroid: Area, line અથવા volume નું geometrical centre; plane area માટે centroidal axes વિશે first moments of area શૂન્ય હોય છે.

Centre of gravity: આપેલા gravitational field માં શરીરના resultant gravitational force (weight) ની ક્રિયારેખા જેમાંથી પસાર થાય તે point.

Axis of symmetry: Plane figure ને બે mirror-image ભાગોમાં વહેંચતી line. Centroid દરેક axis of symmetry પર હોય છે. Uniform gravitational field માં homogeneous body નું centre of gravity તેના centroid પર હોય છે.

Q1(c)7 MARKSState the classification of a force system and explain each type.Force system નું વર્ગીકરણ કરો અને દરેક પ્રકાર સમજાવો.

Force system: A set of forces acting simultaneously on a body. Forces are classified by the locations of their lines of action.

1. Coplanar force systems: All forces lie in one plane.

  • Collinear: All lines of action lie on the same straight line. Example: pulls along one rope.
  • Concurrent: Lines of action meet at one point. Example: several tensions at a pin.
  • Parallel: Lines of action are parallel; they may act in the same or opposite senses. Example: vertical loads on a beam.
  • General coplanar (non-concurrent, non-parallel): Lines neither all meet at one point nor are all parallel. Example: inclined and vertical loads at different beam locations.

2. Non-coplanar (spatial) force systems: Lines of action do not all lie in one plane.

  • Concurrent: All lines pass through one point in space, e.g. guy-wire tensions at a mast joint.
  • Parallel: Parallel forces at different spatial locations, e.g. vertical loads on a three-dimensional frame.
  • General non-concurrent, non-parallel: A spatial force system whose lines are neither all concurrent nor all parallel.

Collinear forces are a special case that may also be described as concurrent or parallel under broader definitions.

Force system: શરીર પર એકસાથે લાગતા forces નો સમૂહ. તેમની lines of action ની સ્થિતિ પ્રમાણે વર્ગીકરણ થાય છે.

1. Coplanar force systems: બધા forces એક plane માં હોય છે.

  • Collinear: બધી lines of action એક જ સીધી રેખા પર હોય છે. ઉદાહરણ: એક દોરી પરના pulls.
  • Concurrent: Lines of action એક point પર મળે છે. ઉદાહરણ: pin પર અનેક tensions.
  • Parallel: Lines of action સમાનાંતર હોય છે; તેમની sense સમાન અથવા વિરુદ્ધ હોઈ શકે. ઉદાહરણ: beam પર vertical loads.
  • General coplanar (non-concurrent, non-parallel): Lines બધી એક point પર મળતી નથી અને બધી parallel પણ નથી. ઉદાહરણ: beam ના જુદા સ્થાનો પર inclined અને vertical loads.

2. Non-coplanar (spatial) force systems: બધી lines of action એક plane માં નથી.

  • Concurrent: બધી lines અવકાશમાં એક point માંથી પસાર થાય છે, જેમ કે mast joint ના guy-wire tensions.
  • Parallel: અલગ spatial locations પર parallel forces, જેમ કે three-dimensional frame પર vertical loads.
  • General non-concurrent, non-parallel: Spatial system જેમાં બધી lines concurrent કે બધી parallel નથી.

Collinear forces એક વિશેષ કેસ છે જેને વ્યાપક વ્યાખ્યામાં concurrent અથવા parallel પણ કહી શકાય.

Q2(b)4 MARKSState and explain the parallelogram law of forces.Forces નો parallelogram law લખો અને સમજાવો.

Statement: If two concurrent forces are represented in magnitude and direction by two adjacent sides of a parallelogram drawn from their common point, their resultant is represented by the diagonal through that point.

Construction: Draw the forces P and Q to a selected force scale from a common point. Complete the parallelogram by drawing parallels. The common-point diagonal gives the magnitude and direction of R.

Analytical form: For included angle θ and resultant angle α measured from P towards Q:

R = √(P² + Q² + 2PQ cos θ)
tan α = Q sin θP + Q cos θ

Choose the appropriate quadrant for α. The law is vector addition and applies to two forces acting at a common point.

નિયમ: એક common point થી દોરેલા parallelogram ની બાજુબાજુની બે sides જો બે concurrent forces ને magnitude અને direction માં દર્શાવે, તો તે point માંથી દોરાયેલ diagonal તેમનો resultant દર્શાવે છે.

Construction: પસંદ કરેલા force scale મુજબ common point થી P અને Q દોરો. Parallel lines દોરી parallelogram પૂર્ણ કરો. Common-point diagonal, R નું magnitude અને direction આપે છે.

Analytical સ્વરૂપ: વચ્ચેનો angle θ અને P થી Q તરફ માપેલો resultant angle α હોય તો:

R = √(P² + Q² + 2PQ cos θ)
tan α = Q sin θP + Q cos θ

α માટે યોગ્ય quadrant પસંદ કરો. આ vector addition નો નિયમ એક common point પર લાગતા બે forces માટે છે.

Q2(b) OR4 MARKSState the triangle law and polygon law of forces.Triangle law અને polygon law of forces લખો.

Triangle law: If two forces are represented in magnitude and direction by two sides of a triangle taken in order, their resultant is represented by the third side taken from the start of the first vector to the end of the second. The opposite-directed third side is the equilibrant.

Polygon law: If several forces are represented in magnitude and direction by successive sides of a polygon taken head-to-tail, their resultant is the closing vector from the starting point to the final point. If the polygon closes, the vector sum is zero. For full rigid-body equilibrium, the resultant moment must also be zero; for concurrent forces this follows when the force sum is zero.

Use: Choose a force scale, draw each vector in sequence and measure the closing vector and its direction.

Triangle law: Triangle ની ક્રમમાં લીધેલી બે sides જો બે forces ને magnitude અને direction માં દર્શાવે, તો પ્રથમ vector ની શરૂઆતથી બીજા vector ના અંત સુધીની ત્રીજી side resultant દર્શાવે છે. તેનાથી વિરુદ્ધ દિશાની side equilibrant છે.

Polygon law: અનેક forces ને polygon ની ક્રમવાર head-to-tail sides વડે magnitude અને direction માં દર્શાવતાં, શરૂઆતના point થી અંતિમ point સુધીનો closing vector resultant છે. Polygon બંધ થાય તો vector sum શૂન્ય છે. સંપૂર્ણ rigid-body equilibrium માટે resultant moment પણ શૂન્ય હોવો જોઈએ; concurrent forces માટે force sum શૂન્ય હોય તો આ શરત પૂર્ણ થાય છે.

ઉપયોગ: Force scale પસંદ કરી દરેક vector ક્રમમાં દોરો અને closing vector તથા તેની direction માપો.

Q2(c) OR7 MARKSState and explain Lami’s theorem and define a free-body diagram.Lami theorem લખી સમજાવો અને free-body diagram વ્યાખ્યાયિત કરો.

Statement: If a body is in equilibrium under exactly three non-parallel, coplanar and concurrent forces, each force is proportional to the sine of the angle between the other two forces.

F₁sin α = F₂sin β = F₃sin γ

Here α is the angle between F₂ and F₃, β is between F₃ and F₁, and γ is between F₁ and F₂.

Explanation: In equilibrium the three force vectors form a closed triangle when drawn head-to-tail. Applying the sine rule to that force triangle gives the above relationship. The triangle’s internal angles are supplementary to the angles between forces; their sines are equal.

Steps:

  1. Isolate the body and draw all three forces on its free-body diagram.
  2. Check that they are coplanar, concurrent and in equilibrium.
  3. Mark the angle between each pair and place each force opposite the sine of the angle between the other two.
  4. Solve for the unknown forces and verify equilibrium.

Free-body diagram (FBD): A sketch of an isolated body showing all external forces and moments acting on it, including applied loads, weight and support reactions. Internal forces are not shown unless a cut exposes them.

નિયમ: જો શરીર ચોક્કસ ત્રણ non-parallel, coplanar અને concurrent forces હેઠળ equilibrium માં હોય, તો દરેક force અન્ય બે forces વચ્ચેના angle ના sine ના પ્રમાણમાં હોય છે.

F₁sin α = F₂sin β = F₃sin γ

અહીં α, F₂ અને F₃ વચ્ચે; β, F₃ અને F₁ વચ્ચે; અને γ, F₁ અને F₂ વચ્ચેનો angle છે.

સમજૂતી: Equilibrium માં ત્રણ force vectors head-to-tail દોરતાં બંધ triangle બનાવે છે. આ force triangle પર sine rule લગાવવાથી ઉપરનો સંબંધ મળે છે. Triangle ના internal angles forces વચ્ચેના angles ના supplementary છે; તેમના sines સમાન છે.

પગલાં:

  1. Body ને અલગ કરી free-body diagram માં ત્રણેય forces દોરો.
  2. તેઓ coplanar, concurrent અને equilibrium માં છે તેની ખાતરી કરો.

Materials can be grouped according to the physical property relevant to their use. These groups overlap; a single material can belong to several.

  • Density: Light materials such as aluminium and polymers; high-density materials such as steel and lead.
  • Electrical conductivity: Conductors (copper, aluminium), semiconductors (silicon) and insulators (ceramics, many polymers).

Statement: Over a suitable operating range, the effort P required to raise load W is often approximated by a linear relation:

P = mW + C

Terms: m is the slope of the effort–load line; C is the intercept, representing the modelled no-load effort. Both depend on the machine and conditions. It is an empirical approximation, not a universal exact law.

Determination: Measure two or more load–effort pairs in steady operation and fit the line. For two points:

m = P₂ − P₁W₂ − W₁
C = P₁ − mW₁

Related quantities:

MA = WP
η = MAVR
η = WVR(mW + C)

If m > 0, C ≥ 0 and VR is constant, the limiting efficiency as W tends to infinity is 1/(m VR), provided the fitted model remains valid. If C = 0, the model gives constant efficiency for every positive load. Real friction, speed and load may change the law outside the tested range.

P = mW + C
