Q1(a)3 MARKSDefine accuracy, least count and calibration.Accuracy, least count અને calibration વ્યાખ્યાયિત કરો.

Accuracy: Closeness of agreement between a measured value and the true or accepted reference value. Accuracy is qualitative; it is not the same as repeatability or resolution.

Least count: The smallest scale increment or indicated change that an instrument can resolve under its design; the actual measurement uncertainty can be larger.

Calibration: Establishing the relationship between instrument indications and reference values with their measurement uncertainties under stated conditions. Calibration does not necessarily include adjustment.

Accuracy: Measured value અને true અથવા accepted reference value વચ્ચે agreement ની નજીકતા. Accuracy ગુણાત્મક છે; તે repeatability અથવા resolution સમાન નથી.

Least count: Instrument ની રચના મુજબ resolve થઈ શકે તેવો સૌથી નાનો scale increment અથવા indicated change; actual measurement uncertainty વધારે હોઈ શકે છે.

Calibration: નિર્ધારિત conditions હેઠળ instrument indications અને reference values વચ્ચેનો સંબંધ તથા તેમની measurement uncertainties સ્થાપિત કરવી. Calibration માં adjustment જરૂરી રીતે સામેલ નથી.

Q1(b)4 MARKSExplain the line standard of measurement.Line standard of measurement સમજાવો.

Definition: A line standard represents length as the distance between the centres of two engraved lines on a graduated scale or bar. Examples are a steel rule and a calibrated graduated scale.

Measurement: Place the scale along the dimension, align one end with a reference graduation and read the graduation at the other end. Use suitable magnification and a perpendicular view to reduce reading error.

Advantages:

  • Quick and convenient for a wide range of lengths.
  • No need for precision contact between the workpiece ends and the scale faces.

Limitations:

  • Accuracy depends on line thickness, graduation accuracy, alignment and parallax.
  • Wear, temperature and imperfect positioning affect the reading; it is generally less precise than suitable end standards such as gauge blocks.

વ્યાખ્યા: Line standard માં length, graduated scale અથવા bar પરની બે કોતરેલી lines ના centres વચ્ચેના distance થી દર્શાવાય છે. Steel rule અને calibrated graduated scale ઉદાહરણો છે.

Measurement: Scale ને dimension ની દિશામાં મૂકી એક છેડો reference graduation સાથે ગોઠવો અને બીજા છેડે graduation વાંચો. Reading error ઘટાડવા યોગ્ય magnification અને perpendicular view રાખો.

ફાયદા:

  • મોટી range ની lengths માટે ઝડપી અને અનુકૂળ.
  • Workpiece ના છેડા અને scale faces વચ્ચે precision contact જરૂરી નથી.

મર્યાદાઓ:

  • Accuracy line thickness, graduation accuracy, alignment અને parallax પર આધારિત છે.
  • Wear, temperature અને અયોગ્ય positioning reading ને અસર કરે છે; યોગ્ય end standards જેમ કે gauge blocks કરતાં સામાન્ય રીતે ઓછી precision મળે છે.
Q1(c)7 MARKSExplain principle, construction and least count of a vernier caliper with a sketch.Vernier caliper નો સિદ્ધાંત, રચના અને least count sketch સાથે સમજાવો.

Principle: Two scales with slightly different division lengths are compared. The vernier division that coincides with a main-scale line gives the fractional part of the main-scale division.

Construction:

  • A graduated beam with a fixed jaw carries the main scale.
  • A sliding jaw and vernier scale move together along the beam; a locking screw holds the setting.
  • Outside jaws measure external dimensions, inside jaws measure internal dimensions, and a depth rod measures depth. Some models have step-measuring faces.

Least count for a direct vernier: If n vernier divisions occupy the same length as (n − 1) main-scale divisions (MSD):

1 VSD = n − 1n × 1 MSD
Least count = 1 MSD − 1 VSD
Least count = 1 MSDn

For example, if 50 vernier divisions span 49 mm and 1 MSD = 1 mm, LC = 0.02 mm. The actual instrument’s graduations determine its LC.

Working / reading:

  1. Clean jaws and workpiece, close jaws and check zero error.
  2. Measure with suitable gentle contact and lock if necessary.
  3. Read the last main-scale mark before vernier zero.
  4. Find the coincident vernier division and multiply its number by LC. Add this to the main reading; apply the zero correction with its sign.

Avoid parallax, excessive jaw force and misalignment. Calibrate and report uncertainty appropriate to the measurement.

સિદ્ધાંત: થોડા જુદા division lengths ધરાવતી બે scales સરખાવવામાં આવે છે. Main-scale line સાથે મળતી vernier division, main-scale division નો fractional ભાગ આપે છે.

રચના:

  • Graduated beam અને fixed jaw પર main scale હોય છે.
  • Sliding jaw અને vernier scale beam પર સાથે ખસે છે; locking screw setting સ્થિર કરે છે.
  • Outside jaws external dimensions, inside jaws internal dimensions અને depth rod depth માપે છે. કેટલાક models માં step-measuring faces હોય છે.

Direct vernier માટે least count: n vernier divisions ની length, (n − 1) main-scale divisions (MSD) જેટલી હોય તો:

1 VSD = n − 1n × 1 MSD
Least count = 1 MSD − 1 VSD
Least count = 1 MSDn

ઉદાહરણ: 50 vernier divisions 49 mm જેટલી અને 1 MSD = 1 mm હોય તો LC = 0.02 mm. વાસ્તવિક instrument ના graduations તેનો LC નક્કી કરે છે.

Working / reading:

  1. Jaws અને workpiece સાફ કરો, jaws બંધ કરી zero error તપાસો.
  2. યોગ્ય હળવા contact સાથે માપી જરૂર હોય તો lock કરો.
  3. Vernier zero પહેલાંનું છેલ્લું main-scale mark વાંચો.
  4. મળતી vernier division શોધી તેના નંબરને LC થી ગુણો. તે main reading માં ઉમેરો; signed zero correction લાગુ કરો.

Parallax, વધુ jaw force અને misalignment ટાળો. Measurement માટે યોગ્ય calibration અને uncertainty ધ્યાનમાં લો.

Q1(c) OR7 MARKSExplain the principle and construction of an autocollimator with a sketch.Autocollimator નો સિદ્ધાંત અને રચના sketch સાથે સમજાવો.

Principle: A collimated beam is reflected from a plane mirror and returns through the same objective. If the mirror tilts by a small angle θ, the reflected beam deflects through 2θ and the image shifts at the focal plane.

x ≈ 2fθ
θ ≈ x2f

Here x is displacement, f objective focal length and θ in radians; the formula is a small-angle approximation.

Construction: Light source and illuminated target graticule; beam splitter; objective lens; plane reflecting mirror attached to the workpiece; eyepiece with measuring graticule or an electronic position detector.

Working:

  1. Place the target at the objective focal plane, producing a parallel outgoing beam.
  2. With the plane mirror normal to the axis, its returned target image coincides with the reference.
  3. A small mirror tilt deflects the returned rays, shifting the image in the focal plane.
  4. Measure displacement with the eyepiece micrometer or detector and convert to angular tilt using the calibrated relation.

Applications: Straightness, flatness, angular alignment and machine-guideway inspection. Accurate setup and reflector stability are important.

સિદ્ધાંત: Collimated beam plane mirror પરથી reflect થઈ એ જ objective માં પરત આવે છે. Mirror નાનો angle θ જેટલો ઝુકે તો reflected beam 2θ જેટલો વળે છે અને focal plane પર image ખસે છે.

x ≈ 2fθ
θ ≈ x2f

અહીં x displacement, f objective focal length અને θ radians માં છે; સૂત્ર small-angle approximation છે.

રચના: Light source અને illuminated target graticule; beam splitter; objective lens; workpiece સાથે જોડાયેલ plane reflecting mirror; measuring graticule ધરાવતું eyepiece અથવા electronic position detector.

Working:

  1. Target ને objective focal plane માં રાખતાં બહાર જતો parallel beam મળે છે.
  2. Plane mirror axis ને normal હોય ત્યારે પાછી આવતી target image reference સાથે મળે છે.
  3. Mirror થોડો ઝુકે ત્યારે પાછા આવતા rays વળે છે અને focal plane માં image ખસે છે.
  4. Eyepiece micrometer અથવા detector થી displacement માપી calibrated relation વડે angular tilt શોધો.

ઉપયોગ: Straightness, flatness, angular alignment અને machine-guideway inspection. યોગ્ય setup અને reflector stability જરૂરી છે.

Q2(c)7 MARKSList methods of testing straightness and explain any one.Straightness ચકાસવાની પદ્ધતિઓ લખો અને કોઈપણ એક સમજાવો.

Methods: Straightedge and feeler gauge/light gap; dial indicator against a suitable reference; precision spirit level; autocollimator with reflector; taut wire/telescope; laser interferometer or calibrated laser alignment system. Selection depends on length, orientation and accuracy.

Precision spirit-level method — apparatus: A calibrated precision level with a base or bridge of known equal spacing l, a clean guideway, and a record of readings.

Procedure:

  1. Clean the test surface and place the level at the first station. Record the reading with the level oriented consistently.
  2. Move the bridge by one station at a time and record each inclination; ensure the base spacing and step correspond.
  3. Convert each reading to angular slope θᵢ using the level calibration and sign convention.
  4. Compute successive height changes and accumulate them to get a profile.
  5. Remove the chosen datum slope, for example the line joining the end points. The range between the highest and lowest residual heights is the straightness deviation for that datum; an accepted minimum-zone evaluation may give a different value.
Δhᵢ ≈ lθᵢ

θᵢ is in radians and the approximation is for small slopes. Reverse readings and calibration can help detect instrument zero error. Temperature, vibration and support influence the result.

પદ્ધતિઓ: Straightedge અને feeler gauge/light gap; યોગ્ય reference સામે dial indicator; precision spirit level; reflector સાથે autocollimator; taut wire/telescope; laser interferometer અથવા calibrated laser alignment system. પસંદગી length, orientation અને accuracy પર આધારિત છે.

Precision spirit-level method — સાધનો: જાણીતા સમાન spacing l ધરાવતા base અથવા bridge સાથે calibrated precision level, સ્વચ્છ guideway અને readings ની નોંધ.

પદ્ધતિ:

  1. Test surface સાફ કરી પ્રથમ station પર level મૂકો. સમાન orientation રાખીને reading નોંધો.
  2. Bridge ને એક station જેટલો ખસેડી દરેક inclination નોંધો; base spacing અને step સુસંગત રાખો.
  3. Level calibration અને sign convention થી દરેક reading ને angular slope θᵢ માં ફેરવો.
  4. દરેક height change ગણી ક્રમશઃ ઉમેરો અને profile મેળવો.
  5. પસંદ કરેલી datum slope દૂર કરો, જેમ કે બંને end points જોડતી line. સૌથી ઊંચા અને નીચા residual heights નો તફાવત આ datum માટે straightness deviation છે; માન્ય minimum-zone evaluation નું value જુદું હોઈ શકે છે.
Δhᵢ ≈ lθᵢ

અહીં θᵢ radians માં છે અને approximation નાના slopes માટે છે. Reverse readings અને calibration થી instrument zero error ઓળખવામાં મદદ મળે છે. Temperature, vibration અને support પરિણામને અસર કરે છે.

Q2(a) OR3 MARKSExplain the working principle of a coordinate measuring machine (CMM).Coordinate measuring machine (CMM) નો working principle સમજાવો.

Principle: A CMM determines the coordinates of points on a component relative to a defined reference frame. Calibrated linear encoders measure probe movement along mutually perpendicular X, Y and Z axes.

Working: A contact trigger/scanning probe or a suitable non-contact sensor samples points. The controller combines axis positions with calibrated probe geometry, constructs geometric features such as planes and circles, and compares dimensions and positions with the drawing or CAD model.

Reference alignment, probe qualification, thermal control and measurement uncertainty are essential for reliable results.

સિદ્ધાંત: CMM નિર્ધારિત reference frame ની સરખામણીએ component પરના points ના coordinates શોધે છે. Calibrated linear encoders પરસ્પર લંબ X, Y અને Z axes પર probe movement માપે છે.

Working: Contact trigger/scanning probe અથવા યોગ્ય non-contact sensor points sample કરે છે. Controller, axis positions ને calibrated probe geometry સાથે જોડી planes અને circles જેવી geometric features બનાવે છે અને dimensions તથા positions ને drawing અથવા CAD model સાથે સરખાવે છે.

વિશ્વસનીય પરિણામ માટે reference alignment, probe qualification, thermal control અને measurement uncertainty જરૂરી છે.

Q2(c) OR7 MARKSList methods of testing flatness and explain any one.Flatness ચકાસવાની પદ્ધતિઓ લખો અને કોઈપણ એક સમજાવો.

Methods: Surface plate with dial indicator; precision level or autocollimator on a grid; optical flat with monochromatic light and interference fringes; straightedge and feeler gauge for preliminary checks; CMM or laser-based surface measurement.

Precision-level grid method:

  1. Clean the supported surface and lay out a grid of equally spaced test lines in two directions.
  2. Move a calibrated precision level on a bridge of known spacing along each grid line, recording the slope at every step.
  3. Convert each slope to a height difference and accumulate readings to obtain profiles. Connect and check the profiles at intersecting stations so the grid is on a common height datum.
  4. Fit or choose the specified reference plane and remove overall tilt; compare the heights above and below that plane.
  5. Evaluate flatness as the separation of two parallel planes enclosing all measured points. The minimum-zone flatness is the smallest such separation; a chosen or fitted plane gives an approximate evaluation unless it achieves that minimum.

Precautions: Maintain constant orientation, support the plate correctly, control temperature and vibration, and allow for instrument calibration and uncertainty.

પદ્ધતિઓ: Surface plate સાથે dial indicator; grid પર precision level અથવા autocollimator; monochromatic light અને interference fringes સાથે optical flat; preliminary check માટે straightedge અને feeler gauge; CMM અથવા laser-based surface measurement.

Precision-level grid method:

  1. યોગ્ય રીતે supported surface સાફ કરી બે દિશામાં સમાન spacing વાળી test lines નો grid બનાવો.
  2. જાણીતા spacing વાળા bridge પર calibrated precision level દરેક grid line પર ખસેડી દરેક step ની slope નોંધો.

Q3(c)7 MARKSExplain construction and working of gear-tooth vernier caliper with a sketch.Gear-tooth vernier caliper ની રચના અને working sketch સાથે સમજાવો.

  1. Determine the theoretical chordal addendum and chordal thickness from the specified module, number of teeth and gear geometry.
  2. Set the vertical scale to chordal addendum and lock it.
  3. Place the depth tongue on the tooth top; gently bring the horizontal jaws into contact with opposite flanks.
  4. Read main and vernier scales, apply zero correction and compare with the drawing/tolerance. Repeat on several teeth.

d = mz
Chordal thickness t = d sin(90°z)
Chordal addendum h = m + d2 [1 − cos(90°z)]

Working:

  1. આપેલ module, teeth ની સંખ્યા અને gear geometry પરથી theoretical chordal addendum તથા chordal thickness શોધો.

  4. Main તથા vernier scales વાંચો, zero correction લગાવો અને drawing/tolerance સાથે સરખાવો. અનેક teeth પર પુનરાવર્તન કરો.

Q3(c) OR7 MARKSExplain the three-wire method of measuring screw thread parameters.Screw thread parameters માપવા three-wire method સમજાવો.

Purpose: The three-wire method determines the effective or pitch diameter of an external thread from a measurement over three equal, calibrated wires. Pitch and flank angle are required or checked independently; one over-wire reading does not independently determine all thread parameters.

Construction and procedure:

  1. Clean the external thread, wires and micrometer anvils; use three equal-diameter precision wires suitable for the pitch.
  2. Place two wires in adjacent grooves on one side and the third in a groove opposite, held with suitable wire holders.
  3. Measure distance M over the wires using a calibrated micrometer and consistent measuring force; avoid tilting and repeat readings.
  4. Calculate pitch diameter E using the wire diameter d and pitch P for the actual thread form. Compare with the specified thread tolerance.

For a symmetrical 60° thread, neglecting helix-angle and deformation corrections:

E = M − 3d + 0.866025P
Best wire diameter dbest = 0.57735P

Best-size wires contact near the pitch line. Account for wire calibration, thread flank-angle/helix effects, contact force and temperature for high-accuracy work; use the appropriate formula for other thread angles.

હેતુ: Three-wire method માં સમાન calibrated ત્રણ wires ઉપરનું માપ લઈ external thread નો effective અથવા pitch diameter શોધાય છે. Pitch અને flank angle જરૂરી છે અથવા અલગથી ચકાસાય છે; એક over-wire reading થી બધા thread parameters અલગથી નક્કી થતા નથી.

રચના અને પદ્ધતિ:

  1. External thread, wires અને micrometer anvils સાફ કરો; pitch માટે યોગ્ય સમાન diameter ના ત્રણ precision wires લો.
  2. એક બાજુ અડોશી grooves માં બે wires અને વિરુદ્ધ બાજુ એક wire યોગ્ય holders વડે મૂકો.
  3. Calibrated micrometer અને સમાન measuring force વડે wires ઉપરનું distance M માપો; ઢળવું ટાળો અને readings પુનરાવર્તિત કરો.
  4. Actual thread form માટે wire diameter d અને pitch P વડે pitch diameter E ગણો. નિર્ધારિત thread tolerance સાથે સરખાવો.

Symmetrical 60° thread માટે, helix-angle અને deformation corrections અવગણતાં:

E = M − 3d + 0.866025P
Best wire diameter dbest = 0.57735P

Best-size wires pitch line નજીક contact કરે છે. ઊંચી accuracy માટે wire calibration, thread flank-angle/helix effects, contact force અને temperature ધ્યાનમાં લો; અન્ય thread angles માટે યોગ્ય formula વાપરો.

Q4(b)4 MARKSExplain Taylor’s principle of gauge design.Gauge design માટે Taylor principle સમજાવો.

Q4(c)7 MARKSDefine transducers and classify them.Transducer ની વ્યાખ્યા આપો અને તેનું વર્ગીકરણ કરો.

Definition: A transducer converts a physical quantity or energy from one form into another usable signal. A measurement transducer commonly converts a measurand such as temperature, force or displacement into an electrical output.

Classification:

  1. By energy supply: Active/self-generating transducers produce an output from the measurand without external excitation, e.g. thermocouple or piezoelectric sensor. Passive transducers need excitation and change an electrical parameter, e.g. strain gauge or RTD.
  2. By output: Analog transducers produce a continuously varying signal; digital transducers produce discrete pulses or coded output, e.g. an incremental encoder.
  3. By transduction principle: Resistive (strain gauge, RTD), inductive (LVDT), capacitive (displacement sensor), piezoelectric, thermoelectric (thermocouple), photoelectric and electromagnetic.
  4. By role: Primary sensing element responds directly to the measurand; a secondary transducer converts its output into a convenient signal. One assembly can perform both roles.
  5. By contact: Contact sensors touch the work; non-contact sensors, such as optical displacement sensors, do not.
  6. By measured quantity: Displacement, force, pressure, temperature, flow, acceleration and others.

Selection depends on range, sensitivity, accuracy, response time, environment, calibration and required signal conditioning.

વ્યાખ્યા: Transducer physical quantity અથવા energy ને એક સ્વરૂપમાંથી બીજા ઉપયોગી signal માં ફેરવે છે. Measurement transducer સામાન્ય રીતે temperature, force અથવા displacement જેવા measurand ને electrical output માં ફેરવે છે.

વર્ગીકરણ:

  1. Energy supply મુજબ: Active/self-generating transducers external excitation વગર measurand થી output આપે છે, જેમ કે thermocouple અથવા piezoelectric sensor. Passive transducers ને excitation જોઈએ અને તેઓ electrical parameter બદલે છે, જેમ કે strain gauge અથવા RTD.
  2. Output મુજબ: Analog transducers સતત બદલાતો signal આપે છે; digital transducers discrete pulses અથવા coded output આપે છે, જેમ કે incremental encoder.
  3. Transduction principle મુજબ: Resistive (strain gauge, RTD), inductive (LVDT), capacitive (displacement sensor), piezoelectric, thermoelectric (thermocouple), photoelectric અને electromagnetic.
  4. ભૂમિકા મુજબ: Primary sensing element સીધો measurand ને પ્રતિસાદ આપે છે; secondary transducer તેના output ને અનુકૂળ signal માં ફેરવે છે. એક assembly બંને ભૂમિકાઓ કરી શકે છે.
  5. Contact મુજબ: Contact sensors work ને સ્પર્શે છે; optical displacement sensor જેવા non-contact sensors સ્પર્શતા નથી.
  6. Measured quantity મુજબ: Displacement, force, pressure, temperature, flow, acceleration વગેરે.

પસંદગી range, sensitivity, accuracy, response time, environment, calibration અને જરૂરી signal conditioning પર આધારિત છે.

Q4(a) OR3 MARKSExplain interchangeability with reference to limits, fits and tolerances.Limits, fits અને tolerances ના સંદર્ભમાં interchangeability સમજાવો.

Interchangeability: Ability to replace one manufactured component with another of the same specification and achieve the required assembly and function without selective fitting or individual rework.

Relationship:

  1. Limits are the maximum and minimum permissible sizes.
  2. Tolerance is the difference between those limits.
  3. A fit is the clearance or interference relationship of mating parts. If each part is made within appropriate limits, the specified fit is maintained across a production batch.

True functional interchangeability also requires geometric tolerances, surface condition, material and other specified requirements, not size alone.

Interchangeability: સમાન specification ધરાવતા એક component ના સ્થાને બીજો component મૂકી selective fitting અથવા individual rework વગર જરૂરી assembly અને function મેળવવાની ક્ષમતા.

સંબંધ:

  1. Limits એટલે maximum અને minimum permissible sizes.
  2. Tolerance એટલે તે limits વચ્ચેનો તફાવત.
  3. Fit એટલે mating parts વચ્ચે clearance અથવા interference નો સંબંધ. દરેક part યોગ્ય limits માં બનાવતાં production batch માં નિર્ધારિત fit જળવાય છે.

સંપૂર્ણ functional interchangeability માટે ફક્ત size નહીં, geometric tolerances, surface condition, material અને અન્ય નિર્ધારિત requirements પણ જરૂરી છે.

Q4(b) OR4 MARKSCompare measurement and gauging.Measurement અને gauging ની સરખામણી કરો.

1. Output: Measurement gives a numerical value with unit; limit gauging gives an accept/reject decision relative to specified limits.

2. Tools: Measurement uses calipers, micrometers or CMMs; gauging uses plug, ring or other GO/NO-GO gauges.

3. Information: Measurement can show magnitude and trend of deviations; a limit gauge does not normally show the actual size.

4. Production: Gauging is often faster for repeated shop-floor checks; measurement is more informative for setup, calibration and investigation.

5. Quality: Both require suitable calibration, correct use and uncertainty control. A gauge decision near a limit can be affected by gauge tolerance and measurement uncertainty.

1. Output: Measurement unit સાથે numerical value આપે છે; limit gauging નિર્ધારિત limits સામે accept/reject નિર્ણય આપે છે.

2. Tools: Measurement માટે calipers, micrometers અથવા CMMs; gauging માટે plug, ring અથવા અન્ય GO/NO-GO gauges વપરાય છે.

3. માહિતી: Measurement deviation નું magnitude અને trend બતાવી શકે છે; limit gauge સામાન્ય રીતે actual size બતાવતું નથી.

4. Production: વારંવારના shop-floor checks માટે gauging ઘણી વાર ઝડપી; setup, calibration અને તપાસ માટે measurement વધુ માહિતી આપે છે.

5. Quality: બંને માટે યોગ્ય calibration, સાચો ઉપયોગ અને uncertainty control જરૂરી છે. Limit નજીક gauge tolerance અને measurement uncertainty નિર્ણયને અસર કરી શકે છે.

Q4(c) OR7 MARKSExplain the principle and measurement applications of a piezoelectric transducer.Piezoelectric transducer નો સિદ્ધાંત અને measurement applications સમજાવો.

Principle: Certain materials such as quartz produce electric charge when mechanically stressed (direct piezoelectric effect). Charge is approximately proportional to applied force within the sensor’s calibrated linear range:

Q = dF

Here Q is charge, F is force and d is an appropriate charge sensitivity coefficient for the crystal and loading direction.

Construction: A piezoelectric element is sandwiched between electrodes in a rigid protective housing. External force is transmitted through a loading member. Shielded leads connect it to a high-input-impedance charge amplifier and display or data-acquisition system.

Working:

  1. The measurand applies force or pressure to the crystal through the mechanical structure.
  2. Mechanical stress produces charge of opposite polarity on the electrode faces.
  3. A charge amplifier converts charge into a proportional voltage; calibration relates output to the input quantity.
  4. The system records dynamic variations with suitable frequency response.

Applications: Dynamic force and pressure, engine-cylinder pressure, impact, vibration and acceleration (accelerometers). Fast response and small size are advantages. Charge leakage makes ordinary piezoelectric sensors unsuitable for long-duration static measurements; temperature and mounting also affect performance.

સિદ્ધાંત: Quartz જેવા કેટલાક materials પર mechanical stress લાગતાં electric charge ઉત્પન્ન થાય છે (direct piezoelectric effect). Calibrated linear range માં charge લગભગ applied force ના પ્રમાણમાં છે:

Q = dF

અહીં Q charge, F force અને d crystal તથા loading direction માટે યોગ્ય charge sensitivity coefficient છે.

રચના: Rigid protective housing માં બે electrodes વચ્ચે piezoelectric element મૂકાય છે. Loading member દ્વારા external force પહોંચે છે. Shielded leads તેને high-input-impedance charge amplifier અને display અથવા data-acquisition system સાથે જોડે છે.

Working:

  1. Mechanical structure દ્વારા measurand crystal પર force અથવા pressure લાગુ કરે છે.
  2. Mechanical stress થી electrodes પર વિરુદ્ધ polarity ના charges બને છે.
  3. Charge amplifier charge ને પ્રમાણસર voltage માં ફેરવે છે; calibration output ને input quantity સાથે સંબંધિત કરે છે.
  4. યોગ્ય frequency response સાથે system dynamic variations નોંધે છે.

ઉપયોગ: Dynamic force અને pressure, engine-cylinder pressure, impact, vibration તથા acceleration (accelerometers). ઝડપી response અને નાનું કદ ફાયદા છે. Charge leakage ને કારણે સામાન્ય piezoelectric sensors લાંબા સમયના static measurement માટે યોગ્ય નથી; temperature અને mounting પણ પરિણામને અસર કરે છે.

Q5(a)3 MARKSDifferentiate a resistance thermometer and thermocouple.Resistance thermometer અને thermocouple વચ્ચે તફાવત લખો.

1. Principle: An RTD uses temperature-dependent electrical resistance, commonly platinum; a thermocouple generates a thermoelectric voltage between junctions of dissimilar metals.

2. Excitation: An RTD needs a measurement current; a thermocouple is self-generating but requires a reference junction or cold-junction compensation.

3. Performance: RTDs often give better stability and accuracy over their rated ranges; thermocouples are rugged, can respond rapidly and selected types cover very high temperatures. The actual range, response and accuracy depend on construction and type.

4. Signal: RTD readout measures resistance; thermocouple output is a small voltage requiring suitable amplification and compensation.

1. સિદ્ધાંત: RTD temperature-dependent electrical resistance વાપરે છે, સામાન્ય રીતે platinum; thermocouple જુદા metals ના junctions વચ્ચે thermoelectric voltage બનાવે છે.

2. Excitation: RTD ને measurement current જોઈએ; thermocouple self-generating છે પણ reference junction અથવા cold-junction compensation જરૂરી છે.

3. Performance: RTDs ઘણી વાર તેમની rated ranges માં સારી stability અને accuracy આપે છે; thermocouples મજબૂત, ઝડપથી પ્રતિસાદ આપતા અને પસંદ કરેલા types ઊંચાં temperatures માટે યોગ્ય છે. Actual range, response અને accuracy construction તથા type પર આધારિત છે.

4. Signal: RTD readout resistance માપે છે; thermocouple નાનું voltage આપે છે જેને યોગ્ય amplification અને compensation જોઈએ.

Q5(b)4 MARKSWrite a short note on digital measurement.Digital measurement પર ટૂંકી નોંધ લખો.

Definition: Digital measurement presents a measured quantity as a discrete numerical value, commonly using an electronic display or computer.

Typical chain: Sensor → signal conditioning → analog-to-digital converter (ADC) → processing → digital display/storage. Some sensors, such as encoders, directly generate digital pulses.

Advantages:

  • Clear numerical readout reduces interpolation and parallax errors.
  • Data can be stored, transmitted, logged and analysed automatically.
  • Good repeatability and resolution are possible with proper design.

Limitations / precautions: ADC quantization, sampling rate, noise, calibration drift and sensor errors affect accuracy. Displayed digits do not themselves establish accuracy; select adequate range, resolution, sampling and calibration.

વ્યાખ્યા: Digital measurement માં measured quantity ને discrete numerical value તરીકે, સામાન્ય રીતે electronic display અથવા computer પર, દર્શાવવામાં આવે છે.

સામાન્ય chain: Sensor → signal conditioning → analog-to-digital converter (ADC) → processing → digital display/storage. Encoder જેવા કેટલાક sensors સીધા digital pulses આપે છે.

ફાયદા:

  • સ્પષ્ટ numerical readout થી interpolation અને parallax errors ઘટે છે.
  • Data store, transmit, log અને automatically analyse કરી શકાય છે.
  • યોગ્ય design થી સારી repeatability અને resolution શક્ય છે.

મર્યાદાઓ / સાવચેતી: ADC quantization, sampling rate, noise, calibration drift અને sensor errors accuracy ને અસર કરે છે. Display પરના digits પોતે accuracy સાબિત કરતા નથી; યોગ્ય range, resolution, sampling અને calibration પસંદ કરો.

Q5(c)7 MARKSExplain construction and working principle of a radiation pyrometer.Radiation pyrometer નો working principle અને construction સમજાવો.

Principle: All objects above absolute zero emit thermal radiation. A radiation pyrometer collects energy from a target and relates the detected radiation to temperature by calibration. A total-radiation ideal blackbody follows the Stefan–Boltzmann relation:

Eb = σT⁴

Eb is blackbody emissive power, σ the Stefan–Boltzmann constant and T absolute temperature. Real surfaces have emissivity less than or equal to 1 and instruments operate over a specified spectral band; practical calibration and emissivity correction are therefore essential.

Construction:

  • Objective lens or mirror collects radiation from a defined spot.
  • A radiation detector, such as a thermopile, converts the received energy into an electrical signal.
  • An amplifier and calibrated electronic indicator/display convert the signal into temperature.
  • The housing, sighting arrangement and sometimes optical filters define the field of view and spectral response.

Working:

  1. Aim at the hot target and ensure the target completely fills the specified measurement spot.
  2. Thermal radiation enters the optics and reaches the detector, which generates an electrical output.
  3. The instrument processes the signal using calibration and emissivity setting, then displays temperature.
  4. Check viewing path, target emissivity, background reflections and distance-to-spot ratio for reliable results.

Applications and limits: Non-contact measurement of furnaces, hot metal and moving objects. It is fast and avoids sensor contact, but emissivity errors, smoke, dust, viewing windows and reflected radiation can cause error.

સિદ્ધાંત: Absolute zero થી ઉપરનાં બધા objects thermal radiation આપે છે. Radiation pyrometer target માંથી energy એકત્ર કરી calibration વડે detected radiation ને temperature સાથે સંબંધિત કરે છે. Total-radiation ideal blackbody માટે Stefan–Boltzmann relation:

Eb = σT⁴

અહીં Eb blackbody emissive power, σ Stefan–Boltzmann constant અને T absolute temperature છે. Real surfaces ની emissivity 1 અથવા તેનાથી ઓછી હોય છે અને instruments નિર્ધારિત spectral band પર કાર્ય કરે છે; તેથી practical calibration અને emissivity correction જરૂરી છે.

રચના:

  • Objective lens અથવા mirror નિર્ધારિત spot માંથી radiation એકત્ર કરે છે.
  • Thermopile જેવા radiation detector મળેલી energy ને electrical signal માં ફેરવે છે.
  • Amplifier અને calibrated electronic indicator/display signal ને temperature માં ફેરવે છે.
  • Housing, sighting arrangement અને ક્યારેક optical filters field of view તથા spectral response નક્કી કરે છે.

Working:

  1. ગરમ target તરફ aim કરો અને નિર્ધારિત measurement spot સંપૂર્ણ target થી ભરાય તેની ખાતરી કરો.
  2. Thermal radiation optics માંથી detector પર પહોંચે છે અને detector electrical output આપે છે.
  3. Instrument calibration તથા emissivity setting સાથે signal process કરી temperature દર્શાવે છે.
  4. વિશ્વસનીય પરિણામ માટે viewing path, target emissivity, background reflections અને distance-to-spot ratio તપાસો.

ઉપયોગ અને મર્યાદા: Furnaces, hot metal અને moving objects નું non-contact measurement. તે ઝડપી છે અને sensor contact ટાળે છે, પરંતુ emissivity errors, smoke, dust, viewing windows અને reflected radiation થી error થઈ શકે છે.

Q5(c) OR7 MARKSWrite a short note on the role of IoT in quality control.Quality control માં IoT ની ભૂમિકા પર ટૂંકી નોંધ લખો.

Meaning: Industrial IoT links sensors, measuring instruments and machines to software systems so quality-related data can be collected, analysed and acted on throughout production.

Role and workflow:

  1. Collect: Connected instruments measure dimensions, temperature, vibration or process parameters; identify the part, instrument and timestamp.
  2. Transmit: A gateway or edge system sends validated data securely to a quality platform, historian or cloud.
  3. Analyse: Dashboards, statistical process control and analytics identify trends, process drift and out-of-control conditions.
  4. Respond: Alerts prompt inspection or maintenance; validated closed-loop controls can adjust the process within approved limits.
  5. Trace: Link results to batch, machine, operator, calibration status and inspection history for traceability and root-cause analysis.
  6. Improve: Aggregate data to support predictive maintenance, reduce scrap and verify corrective action.

Benefits: Faster detection, less manual transcription, visibility across the line and better documented decisions.

Safeguards: Calibrate instruments, manage uncertainty, validate data and timestamps, secure access and networks, maintain local operation during outages, and require human or validated automated approval for consequential adjustments. IoT connectivity alone does not guarantee product quality.

અર્થ: Industrial IoT sensors, measuring instruments અને machines ને software systems સાથે જોડે છે, જેથી production દરમિયાન quality સંબંધિત data એકત્ર, analyse અને તેના આધારે action લઈ શકાય.

ભૂમિકા અને workflow:

  1. Collect: Connected instruments dimensions, temperature, vibration અથવા process parameters માપે છે; part, instrument અને timestamp ઓળખે છે.
  2. Transmit: Gateway અથવા edge system validated data સુરક્ષિત રીતે quality platform, historian અથવા cloud સુધી મોકલે છે.
  3. Analyse: Dashboards, statistical process control અને analytics trends, process drift તથા out-of-control conditions ઓળખે છે.
  4. Respond: Alerts inspection અથવા maintenance સૂચવે છે; validated closed-loop controls મંજૂર limits માં process સુધારી શકે છે.
  5. Trace: Results ને batch, machine, operator, calibration status અને inspection history સાથે જોડવાથી traceability અને root-cause analysis શક્ય બને છે.
  6. Improve: એકત્રિત data વડે predictive maintenance, scrap ઘટાડવું અને corrective action ની પુષ્ટિ શક્ય બને છે.

ફાયદા: ઝડપી detection, ઓછું manual transcription, સમગ્ર line ની visibility અને સારી રીતે દસ્તાવેજીકૃત decisions.

સુરક્ષા: Instruments calibrate કરો, uncertainty સંભાળો, data અને timestamps validate કરો, access તથા networks સુરક્ષિત રાખો, outage દરમિયાન local operation જાળવો અને મહત્વપૂર્ણ adjustments માટે human અથવા validated automated approval રાખો. માત્ર IoT connectivity product quality ની ખાતરી આપતી નથી.
